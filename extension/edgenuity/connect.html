LOCKIN

Connect Edgenuity

LockIn can read how much of a course you have finished from Edgenuity pages you open yourself — a percentage and an activity count, nothing else. It never asks for your password, never changes anything in Edgenuity, and never loads an Edgenuity page on its own.

Edgenuity

edgenuity.com

  • Reads only Edgenuity, and only pages you open yourself
  • Stays switched off during quizzes, tests and exams
  • Stores two numbers per course — never your work, answers or scores
  • Everything stays on this computer

You can remove this access at any time from LockIn Settings or from chrome://extensions.
