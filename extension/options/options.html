Where settings live

Blocked sites, allowed school sites, reminder timing and Focus Mode rules are all part of your LockIn data, so they are edited in the app rather than here. That keeps one copy of every setting instead of two that can disagree.

What this extension can see

  • The address of the tab you are looking at — the site name only, to tell school work from distractions. Never the page, never what you type, never a history.
  • Which sites you asked to block, so it can block them in every Chrome window of this profile.
  • Your assignment titles and due times, so a reminder can say what is due while LockIn is closed.
  • Your Canvas calendar feed address, if you connected one. It is kept here and never handed back to the web page.

What it cannot see

  • Anything in another Chrome profile — including a school profile. Chrome keeps profiles apart and LockIn does not try to get around that.
  • Page contents, form fields, passwords, messages or documents.
  • Your browsing history. Block counts are per-site totals, nothing more.

Permissions

…

Broad site access is required by Chrome for the blocking rules to work at all; it is not used to read anything. Removing it turns blocking off — do it from chrome://extensions if you want that.

Reset

Clears this extension's own storage — block counters, the reminder schedule, activity counters, and the Canvas feed address. Your assignments live in LockIn and are not touched.
